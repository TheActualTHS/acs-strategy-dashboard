9 Exact Failure Points — Click Each

Every crack. Exposed.

01
The hype-to-habit collapse
Week 3 is where 80% of dropout decisions are made
▼

Students enroll at peak emotional energy. By Week 3 the routine sets in. Doubt replies slow. The emotional peak evaporates. The gap between enrollment promise and Week 3 reality is where the decision happens silently. They stop logging in.

The Fix

Lock the schedule. Classes unlock on fixed days. Daily 7AM push with yesterday's score and today's specific target. At Week 4: survey every student — "Is this what you expected?" Every below-70% student gets a personal call. That call recovers 40% of at-risk students.

02
Selling access, not outcomes
A content library creates zero obligation to show up
▼

10MS, Shikho, every platform with high dropout sells access to content. Access creates zero obligation. Student pays ৳1,000, gets a library, feels no pressure to enter it. When life gets busy, the library waits. It waits forever.

The Fix

Sell Day 1 to Day 450. Not access — a 15-month journey with a finish line announced on Day 1. Students committed to a structured program do not passively drift away. They would have to actively abandon a mission.

03
"Genaral Math" on a live ৳8,250 checkout
Zero quality gate between instructor and live page
▼

Spelling errors on live checkout pages. A typo tells every student's subconscious: no senior person reviewed this. If the checkout has no quality control, the course might not either. Premium brands are defined by the floor of their worst moments.

The Fix

Three-gate pipeline: Instructor → Subject Editor → Brand Auditor → Live. One Brand Auditor. One job. Cost: ৳20,000/month. Return: every page a student sees signals premium.

04
Invisible progress
Students feel the same on Day 90 as Day 1
▼

A student watches 30 classes. Nothing in their environment reflects growth. No rank. No streak. No visual. They feel as lost as Day 1 except ৳3,000 poorer and increasingly behind. Shame and invisible stagnation are the two fastest dropout triggers.

The Fix

Battle Profile. Every student gets a public profile: days enrolled (only goes up), MCQ accuracy trend graphed, rank among 15,000, subjects completed. Shareable. Quitting becomes a public act, not a private one. Cost: ৳0.

05
Doubt system bottleneck
18-hour waits exactly when students need confirmation most
▼

One or two instructors fielding questions from 11,000+ students is not a system. It collapses the moment new cycle enrollment begins. Students feel abandoned precisely when they need confirmation to continue.

The Fix

Tier it: 50 Senior Mentors handle Tier 1 in 2 hours. Moderators handle Tier 2 in 24 hours. Instructors handle Tier 3 only. Published SLA: "Your doubt answered in 2 hours." That promise kept consistently is worth more than 10 new instructors.

06
No recovery protocol
5 missed days = silent permanent exit. Nobody calls.
▼

Student misses 5 days. Platform does nothing. Student feels the gap is unrecoverable. Shame compounds. They don't reach out. They disappear. Nobody calls. Platform records them as a statistic.

The Fix

The Rescue Protocol. Day 3 of absence: Senior Mentor sends — "You're 3 days behind. Here is a 20-minute plan to get current by Sunday." Recovers 40% of at-risk students before the dropout decision. 2,400 students per cycle saved by one message at ৳0 cost.

07
Teacher dependency = fragility
BlackBoard Edu split already proved this vulnerability
▼

Abhi Datta Tushar, Kazi Rakibul, Apurbo Opu, Hasnat Shuvro, Hemel Barua — all genuine assets. But when students are loyal to a person and that person leaves, the revenue walks out with them. You cannot poach a system. You can only poach a person.

The Fix

Legacy Series: each instructor records their best explanation of every core concept once. Permanent curriculum — refined, not replaced. Day-to-day delivery uses pre-built slides. Instructor departure no longer collapses the program.

08
Lengthy classes, low content density
The syllabus needs depth. Attention does not follow length.
▼

Significant class time is spent writing questions by hand on screen — questions that already exist in the OneShot slides. Estimated 1 hour wasted per chapter, 18 hours per subject across all cycles. A student who checks out at Minute 25 of a 90-minute class retains less than one who is fully engaged for 35 minutes.

The Fix

35-minute class cap with pre-built slides. Abhi's Polynomial OneShot is already 204 pages. Kazi Rakibul's Probability is 125 pages. These slides become the academic standard. For complex topics: Part A (35 min theory) + Part B (35 min application). Same content. Double engagement.

The Narrative for Students

"The student who watched 90-minute videos felt productive. You finished 35 minutes and actually learned something. That is the difference."

09
Live vs recorded — ACS isn't making the decision
Students left to choose always choose comfort. Comfort has 3× higher dropout rate.
▼

Students choose based on convenience, not effectiveness. Recorded feels comfortable. Live produces 3× better completion rates. ACS lets students make this decision alone. That is a retention failure built into the product design.
